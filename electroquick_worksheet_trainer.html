1. Ohm's law (Ohmsches Gesetz)

U = R × IVoltage = resistance × current

Volt (V) = Ohm (Ω) × Ampere (A)

I = U ÷ RCurrent = voltage ÷ resistance

A = V ÷ Ω

R = U ÷ IResistance = voltage ÷ current

Ω = V ÷ A

2. Electrical power (Leistung)

P = U × IPower = voltage × current

Watt (W) = Volt (V) × Ampere (A)

3. Electrical energy / work (Energie & elektrische Arbeit)

W = P × tWork / energy = power × time

Watt-second (Ws) or Joule (J)

1 Ws = 1 JWatt-second and Joule are equivalent units

Time t is in seconds (s).

In your worksheet, the symbol W is used for electrical work (Arbeit). The unit is Ws or J. Do not confuse the symbol W for work with the unit watt (W) for power.

5. Basic theory from your worksheet

Voltage U — SpannungMeasured in volt (V). It is the electrical potential difference between two points. The voltage arrow points from plus to minus in the worksheet.
Resistance R — WiderstandMeasured in ohm (Ω). 1 Ω means 1 V is needed for a current of 1 A: 1 Ω = 1 V / 1 A.
Current I — StromMeasured in ampere (A). It is the flow of electric charge.
DC — GleichstromDirect current flows in one direction. A battery supplies DC; in the worksheet its symbol is a straight line / DC symbol.
Current direction: Technical current direction is from plus to minus. Physical electron flow is from minus to plus. Your sheet shows both conventions.

6. Measuring correctly (Prüf- und Messgeräte)

Ammeter — StrommessgerätMeasure current in series (in Reihe). The circuit must be opened to insert it. It has very low internal resistance.
Voltmeter — SpannungsmessgerätMeasure voltage in parallel (parallel). It has very high internal resistance.
MultimeterCan measure several electrical quantities, depending on the selected mode and connection.
Other devices shownTwo-pole voltage tester (zweipoliger Spannungsprüfer), clamp meter (Zangenamperemeter), VDE tester (VDE-Prüfgerät).
